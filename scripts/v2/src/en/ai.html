Where clients usually start.

The first workflow should be common, repetitive and easy to check. These five come up most.

  • Customer questions on your website and WhatsApp

    An assistant that answers from your own content, in your tone, and passes the conversation to a person when it can't help.

  • Lead capture and follow-up

    Every enquiry is recorded, qualified and answered on time, with the details your sales team needs already in the CRM.

  • Document and invoice processing

    Invoices, forms and contracts are read, checked and entered into your systems, with anything unusual flagged for review.

  • Internal knowledge assistant

    Your staff ask questions in plain language and get answers from your policies, manuals and past work, with the source shown.

  • Workflow automation

    The hand-offs between tools and teams that people do by copy and paste, done automatically and logged.
