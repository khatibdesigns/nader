What we believe.

The gap is never the model.

When an AI project stalls, the model is rarely the reason. Nobody agreed which decisions AI may make. Nobody owned it when it was wrong. Nobody decided what to build first.

  • Agree what AI may decide
  • Name an owner for when it is wrong
  • Pick the first thing to build

A pilot that cannot go live is a slide.

A demo on clean data proves little. We build pilots on your real systems, so the step after the pilot is going live, not starting again.

  • Real systems, real data
  • A fixed price and a fixed date
  • Something running at the end

Build what you teach, teach what you build.

Nader teaches what the team ships, and the team ships what he teaches. Our own products are where we test it first.

  • Talks drawn from live systems
  • Our products as the test bed
  • Lessons written up in Insights
