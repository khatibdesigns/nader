Formats.

Each one is shaped around the room: what the audience already knows and what they need to decide next.

Keynote

A talk of 30 to 60 minutes that gives a mixed audience a working picture of where AI is useful today and where it is not.

  • 30 to 60 minutes
  • Live examples from real systems
  • Questions from the floor

University lecture

A lecture or guest session for students and faculty that connects the theory to how AI is built and governed in practice.

  • Single lecture or a short series
  • Undergraduate, graduate or executive programmes
  • Theory tied to live systems

Workshop

A half-day or full-day working session where a team maps its own processes and leaves with a short list of AI use cases and the rules for each.

  • Half day or full day
  • Built on your team's real workflows
  • A short list of use cases to act on

Executive briefing

A private, focused session for a board or leadership team on AI strategy, risk and governance, with time for the questions they would not ask in public.

  • Board or leadership team
  • Strategy, risk and governance
  • Private session

Mentoring

Regular sessions with founders and product teams who are building with AI and want a practitioner to check their thinking.

  • Founders and product leads
  • Product, architecture and go-to-market
  • Ongoing, at a pace you choose

Speaker kit.

Short bio

Nader Alkhatib is the founder and CEO of Khatib Designs, an AI and digital transformation consultancy founded in Sofia in 2015. He has run AI in production since 2017 and speaks and lectures on applied AI for leadership teams, universities and founders.

Long bio

Nader Alkhatib is the founder and CEO of Khatib Designs, an AI and digital transformation consultancy founded in Sofia, Bulgaria, in 2015 that serves clients worldwide. He has more than 13 years in engineering and technology leadership, leading organisations of hundreds of engineers and helping businesses reach their goals, from strategy and consulting to raising investment. He has run AI in production since 2017, starting with a computer-vision scale that tracks food waste for a US startup, and his company now runs on AI agents his team built. He holds a degree in computer engineering and an Executive MBA. He speaks, lectures and mentors on applied AI.
