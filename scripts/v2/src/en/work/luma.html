LUMA: glow up, play on.

LUMA is a glowing 40-level puzzle game with a boss fight every five levels, a new daily challenge, six unlockable mascots and a cosmetics shop. No account and no sign-up: open it and play.

ClientKhatib Designs (own title)
MarketWorldwide
Launched2026
PlatformiOS, Android

The challenge

Shipping a game is not the hard part. Shipping one to both stores with working monetisation is: two review processes with different rules, two in-app purchase systems to configure and test, ratings and privacy declarations on each side, and a build that has to survive all of it.

What we built

A cross-platform game with 40 hand-crafted levels, eight boss encounters, a rotating daily challenge, six collectible mascots, three power-ups and a life system that refills over time.

Monetisation is optional throughout: gem packs, a starter bundle and remove-ads. The game is designed to be fair without them.

Key features

  • 40 levels, 8 boss fights
  • A new daily challenge
  • 6 unlockable mascots
  • Optional in-app purchases

The outcome

LUMA passed review and shipped to both the App Store and Google Play, with in-app purchases and ads live on each.

The release pipeline built for it, covering signing, metadata, screenshots and submission, is now reused for every title that follows.
