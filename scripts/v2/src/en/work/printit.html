PrintIt: printing, delivered to your door.

PrintIt handles everyday printing from a phone. Users upload files and specify the job (prints, posters, business cards, folds), get instant quotes from nearby print shops on a live map, and have the finished work delivered.

ClientPrintIt
CountryKuwait
Launched2018
PlatformiOS, Android

The challenge

A two-sided printing marketplace has to turn messy real-world print specifications into instant, comparable quotes, then match each job to the right nearby shop and coordinate delivery.

What we built

We designed and built native iOS and Android apps with file upload and instant quoting, a full job specification builder, a map of nearby print shops and doorstep delivery.

Key features

  • Upload and instant quote
  • Nearby print-shop map
  • Full job specification builder
  • Doorstep delivery

The outcome

PrintIt launched on iOS and Android, bringing on-demand printing to Kuwait.
