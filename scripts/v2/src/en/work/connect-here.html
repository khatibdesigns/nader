Connect Here: meet the people in the room.

Connect Here builds social networking around physical places. Walk into a café, restaurant or bar and the app shows who else is checked in, so you can browse profiles, send a request and start a conversation with people in the same room.

ClientConnect Here LLC
CountryUnited States
Launched2024
PlatformiOS, Android

The challenge

Turning a "who's here right now" idea into something people trust takes careful UX. The product needed real-time presence, clear consent and privacy, and a check-in flow simple enough to use the moment you sit down, so that nearby discovery feels inviting rather than intrusive.

What we built

We designed and built the full product, end to end. The interface is built around three core flows: find a venue, see who is checked in, and connect and chat. It has rich profile cards, connection requests and in-venue messaging.

The apps were built natively for iOS and Android and shipped to both the App Store and Google Play.

Key features

  • Find nearby establishments
  • Real-time "who's here" presence
  • Connect and chat requests
  • In-venue messaging

The outcome

Connect Here is live on the App Store and Google Play.
