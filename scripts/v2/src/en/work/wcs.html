WCS: run the factory floor.

WCS is a management system for steel factories in the United States. Supervisors view timesheets and wages, manage employees and projects, track budgets and chat live with operation managers on site, so every shift stays in sync.

ClientWCS Management
CountryUnited States
Launched2018
PlatformiOS, Android

The challenge

Enterprise operations are unforgiving. Data has to be accurate, role-based and usable on a noisy factory floor. The app needed to bring timesheets, projects and budgets together into something a supervisor could act on in seconds.

What we built

We designed and built native iOS and Android apps with timesheets and wages, project and task tracking, budget reporting and on-site live chat between supervisors and operation managers.

Key features

  • Timesheets and wages
  • Project and task tracking
  • On-site live chat
  • Budget reporting

The outcome

WCS shipped to iOS and Android and runs operations for steel factories in the United States.
