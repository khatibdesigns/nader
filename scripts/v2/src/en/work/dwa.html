DWA: your pharmacy, delivered.

DWA is a pharmacy delivery system that gathers products from nearby pharmacies into browsable categories. Patients search, scan a prescription, order, track and receive their medicine at the door, in Arabic or English, on mobile and web.

ClientDWA
CountryKuwait
Launched2018
PlatformiOS, Android, web

The challenge

Healthcare delivery is sensitive, and in Kuwait it has to be bilingual. We had to bring together inventory from several pharmacies, handle prescription uploads, and deliver a fully right-to-left Arabic experience alongside English.

What we built

We designed and built native iOS and Android apps plus a companion web app, with nearby-pharmacy aggregation, prescription scanning, live order tracking and full Arabic and English localisation.

Key features

  • Nearby-pharmacy aggregation
  • Prescription scanning
  • Live order tracking
  • Arabic and English

The outcome

DWA launched on iOS, Android and the web, delivering medicine across Kuwait.
