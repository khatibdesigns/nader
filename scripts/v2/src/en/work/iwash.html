iWASH: book a wash, get on shining.

iWASH brought Kuwait's car wash market on demand. Drivers browse the washes available nearby, compare ratings and prices, pick an open time slot and book, then track the job to the finish from their phone.

ClientiWASH
CountryKuwait
Launched2018
PlatformiOS, Android

The challenge

A real-time booking marketplace needs accurate availability across many vendors, clear pricing, and a checkout local customers trust. In Kuwait that means both cash and KNET.

What we built

We designed and built native iOS and Android apps with real-time slot booking, ratings and reviews, an in-app coupon wallet and KNET or cash checkout, in a clean, fast interface.

Key features

  • Real-time slot booking
  • Ratings and reviews
  • Cash and KNET checkout
  • In-app coupon wallet

The outcome

iWASH launched on iOS and Android, bringing on-demand car washing to Kuwait.
