Safey: a vault that fights back.

Safey keeps documents, photos and passwords in an encrypted vault on your own device. What makes it different is what happens when someone tries to force it open: it photographs them, and it gives you a second passcode that opens a decoy.

ClientKhatib Designs (own product)
MarketWorldwide
Version3.5, 2026
PlatformiOS

The challenge

A locked app is only as strong as what happens when somebody makes you unlock it. Most vault apps solve encryption and stop there. That leaves the one scenario that actually frightens people, being handed your own phone and told to open it, unaddressed.

What we built

A vault encrypted on the device with AES-256, so nothing readable ever leaves the phone. Failed unlock attempts are photographed silently.

A separate decoy passcode opens an empty vault and sends you an alert, so complying under pressure costs you nothing.

Key features

  • AES-256 encryption on the device
  • Break-in photo alerts
  • Duress decoy passcode
  • Encrypted cloud sync

The outcome

Safey is live on the App Store and has passed App Review through several releases. It is now at version 3.5.
