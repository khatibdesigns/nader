Coines: crypto exchange, simplified.

Coines makes crypto exchange simple. Users register, link a wallet and start exchanging in minutes, with secure peer-to-peer transfers, live status tracking and built-in messaging between traders.

ClientCoines
CountryBulgaria
Launched2018
PlatformiOS, Android

The challenge

Fintech lives and dies on trust. Every transfer had to feel secure and easy to read: a clear exchange status, safe wallet linking, and direct contact between the two sides of a trade, without overwhelming newcomers.

What we built

We designed and built native iOS and Android apps for peer-to-peer exchange, with secure wallet linking, real-time exchange status and trader-to-trader messaging.

Key features

  • Peer-to-peer exchange
  • Secure wallet linking
  • Live exchange status
  • Trader messaging

The outcome

Coines shipped to iOS and Android, bringing simple peer-to-peer crypto exchange to its market.
