Website Cost in Kuwait: Two Fixed Prices, Not Ranges

29 October 2026 · 4 min read · Khatib Designs

Almost nobody publishes a website cost for Kuwait, because publishing one means committing to a scope. Here are both of ours: KWD 1,850 for a single landing page live in 7 days, and KWD 3,900 for a multi-page site with Arabic and full RTL support in 3 weeks. Read the scope, then decide — there is no discovery call before you see the number.

Why most website quotes in Kuwait arrive as a range

Ask five studios what a website costs in Kuwait and you will get five ranges — usually something like KWD 400 to KWD 6,000 — plus a question about your budget before anything else. That is not dishonesty; it is a scoping problem. At the time of the first call nobody knows how many pages you need, whether Arabic is in scope, who writes the copy, or how many rounds of review you will want. So the number stays open, and the project quietly absorbs whatever happens next.

We price from the other direction. Two shapes cover roughly nine out of ten website requests that reach us from Kuwait and the wider GCC, so we fixed the price of both and wrote the scope down instead of the number. These are fixed prices, not estimates. If the scope below matches what you need, the figure you read is the figure you pay.

Option A — single landing page, live in 7 days, KWD 1,850

One page, one purpose: a visitor arrives from an ad, a WhatsApp forward or a search result, understands the offer, and contacts you. This is the right option if you are validating a service, launching one product, or replacing an Instagram bio link that is doing work it was never built for.

What the KWD 1,850 includes:

  • One long-form page: hero, offer, proof, pricing or enquiry block, FAQ, contact
  • English copy written by us from a 45-minute call — you are not asked to deliver text
  • Mobile-first build, tested on the phones people in Kuwait actually use
  • WhatsApp click-to-chat as the primary action, with the message prefilled
  • Analytics and conversion tracking wired before launch, not after
  • Two revision rounds, then live on your domain within seven working days

What it does not include: Arabic, a blog, logins, payments, or more pages. Those are Option B. If Option A is the fit, send us Option A on WhatsApp and we will send back the start date. The seven-day process itself is written up in how a landing page ships in 7 days.

Option B — multi-page with Arabic and RTL, 3 weeks, KWD 3,900

A real site: five to eight pages, both languages, and a layout that genuinely mirrors rather than one that pastes Arabic into a left-to-right grid. Right-to-left typesetting changes navigation order, icon direction, form alignment and line breaking — it is build work, not a translation task, which is why it sits in the price rather than beside it.

What the KWD 3,900 includes:

  • Five to eight pages: home, services, about, case studies or products, contact
  • Full Arabic and English, each with its own URL structure and metadata
  • Proper RTL implementation, with Arabic type chosen for screen reading
  • A content editor your team can use without calling us
  • Technical SEO on both languages: canonicals, hreflang, sitemap, structured data
  • Three revision rounds, live in three weeks

A bilingual marketplace with live quoting is the closest thing we have built to this — see the PrintIt build for how the two languages sit side by side in production. To start, message us Option B on WhatsApp.

What moves the price, and what does not

Three things move it: logins and user accounts, payment collection, and anything that needs a database of your own records. Each of those turns a website into an application, and applications are priced separately — our published figures for that work sit in design and development pricing for Kuwait.

What does not move it: how many revisions you use inside the included rounds, how long your feedback takes, how many stakeholders join the call, or whether you add a sixth page to an Option B build. We would rather absorb that than re-open a number you have already approved. If you want an assistant or automation layer on top of the site — booking, triage, quoting in Arabic — that is a separate scope on our AI build page.

What it costs after launch

A website is not a one-time payment, and pretending otherwise is how businesses end up with a dead site two years later. Hosting, domain, certificate renewal, dependency updates and content changes all continue. Budget for them deliberately: we break the real numbers down in what maintenance actually costs in the GCC, and the same logic applies to a site as to an app. Performance also decays as content is added, so measure it against published performance guidance rather than how fast it feels on your own phone.

Neither option requires a retainer. You own the code, the domain and the accounts on day one, and you can take the whole thing to another team the week after launch if you want to.

Frequently asked questions

KWD 1,850 for a single page? I was quoted KWD 300.

Both numbers are real, for different things. A KWD 300 page is usually a template with your logo dropped in, your own text, and no tracking — fine if you need a placeholder. KWD 1,850 buys written copy, a conversion-tested structure, analytics wired before launch, and a fixed seven-day delivery date. If a placeholder is genuinely what you need, take the cheaper option; we would rather say that than sell you the wrong tier.

Can I pay in stages instead of all at once?

Yes. Both options split 50% at start and 50% at launch. There is no interest, no financing paperwork, and no change to the total — the price is the price whether you pay once or twice. We do not start before the first payment clears, because that is the only thing protecting the fixed date.

What if my project needs more than Option B? Does the price jump?

It becomes a separate quote, and we tell you that in the first call rather than at week two. Logins, payments and custom databases are application work, not website work. What will not happen is an Option B that starts at KWD 3,900 and arrives at KWD 6,000 — if the scope changes, we price the change in writing before any of it is built.

Are there hidden costs after launch — hosting, domain, maintenance?

Not hidden, but they are real and they are not in the build price. Expect roughly KWD 15 to 40 a year for a domain and KWD 10 to 60 a month for hosting, depending on traffic. Maintenance is optional and charged by the hour, not by retainer. We list these before you sign so the number you approve is the number you spend.
