Landing Page Design Kuwait: KWD 350, Live in 7 Days

26 October 2026 · 4 min read · Khatib Designs

Most landing page design in Kuwait gets quoted the way a full website does — a discovery call, a proposal, a number somewhere between KWD 800 and KWD 2,500, and six weeks of revisions. For a single page with a single job, that is the wrong shape. So we fixed it: one bilingual page, KWD 350, live in seven working days, 50% deposit to start.

What KWD 350 includes

The scope is fixed before you pay, and it is deliberately narrow. One page, in Arabic and English, built to carry one action.

  • One bilingual page — a real right-to-left Arabic layout, not an English page with the text flipped.
  • Up to six sections: hero, proof, the offer, objections, a short FAQ, and one closing call to action.
  • One action only — WhatsApp, a form, or a booking link. Not three buttons competing for the same click.
  • Copy in both languages, written by us from a 30-minute intake call and approved by you before build.
  • Mobile-first build on your own domain, aiming to pass Core Web Vitals on a mid-range Android phone over Kuwaiti mobile data.
  • Lead handoff wired up — form submissions to an inbox you name, or a pre-filled WhatsApp message to your number.
  • Your existing Meta or Google tag installed, so the page is measurable from the first day of traffic.
  • One round of revisions inside the seven days.

What the seven days actually look like

Seven working days, and the clock only moves when approvals come back the same day. That is the one thing we need from you.

  • Day 1 — 30-minute intake call: the one action, the audience, and the proof we are allowed to show.
  • Days 2 and 3 — Arabic and English copy, sent as one document for one approval.
  • Days 4 and 5 — design and build, both language versions.
  • Day 6 — forms, tracking, speed work, and an Arabic proofread by a native reader.
  • Day 7 — your review, then launch on your domain.

Seven days is honest here because nothing on that list has to be invented. Builds that depend on data, models or integrations slip for structural reasons rather than lazy ones — our note on what an AI project timeline really looks like explains why those cannot be promised in a week while a landing page can.

What is not included

We would rather quote these separately than bury them in a bundle you did not ask for.

  • A second page. Two or more pages is a website — different scope, different number, published in our design and development pricing for Kuwait.
  • Logo or brand identity. We work with the brand you already have.
  • Photography or video. Bring your own assets, or we use licensed stock.
  • Checkout or a payment gateway. That is a store, not a landing page.
  • An AI assistant on the page. A chatbot or booking agent is a separate build with its own monthly bill — read what AI running costs come to per month before you add one.
  • Ads management. We build the page the traffic lands on; we do not run the campaign.

The commercial terms are the same ones we apply to every engagement: 50% deposit (KWD 175) reserves the week, the balance is due on launch, and there is no retainer and no lock-in afterwards. The reasoning is in what we charge, in fixed terms, with no retainers.

The bilingual half that most pages get wrong

A homepage has to serve everyone, so it converts nobody in particular. A landing page serves one campaign — and in Kuwait it has to serve it in the language the click arrived in. The expensive version of this mistake is running an Arabic ad to an English page. Nielsen Norman Group research puts the average page visit at well under a minute, with the stay-or-leave decision made in the first 10 to 20 seconds. An Arabic visitor makes that decision on a layout that is still deciding which way to read.

So the Arabic version is not a translation pass at the end. Text direction and alignment, numeral style, form label order, the WhatsApp message pre-filled in Arabic, date and currency written the way a Kuwaiti reader expects — all of it is built, not retrofitted. PrintIt is not a landing page; it is a print marketplace with live quoting. But it had the same constraint: one clear action, and Arabic treated as a first-class layout rather than a translation layer.

If the one action on your page should be an AI assistant that qualifies the lead rather than a form that sits in an inbox, that is a different piece of work. Our AI consultancy and build work covers it, and it is quoted on its own terms.

How to start

Email hello@khatibdesigns.com with three things: your domain, the one action the page has to get, and the date you need it live. We answer with a yes or a no on that date — if the week is already booked we will say so rather than quietly take the deposit. KWD 175 confirms the slot.

Frequently asked questions

Is KWD 350 the final price for a landing page in Kuwait?

Yes, for the scope above: one bilingual page, up to six sections, one action, one revision round, launched on your domain within seven working days. The only way the number changes is if you ask for something on the not-included list, and we quote that separately before any work starts.

What happens if we miss the seven days?

The seven days are working days and they only advance when your approvals come back the same day. If we are the reason for the delay, you do not pay the balance until the page is live. If approvals stall on your side, the week pauses and we reschedule the remaining days with you.

Do we have to keep paying you after launch?

No. There is no retainer. You own the page and the domain. Hosting and your own ad spend are the only recurring costs, and if you later add an AI assistant to the page that carries its own monthly running cost, which we quote up front.
