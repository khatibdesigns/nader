The challenge

Fintech lives and dies on trust. Every transfer had to feel secure and legible: clear exchange status, safe wallet linking, and direct communication between counterparties — without overwhelming newcomers.

What we built

We designed and built native iOS and Android apps for peer-to-peer exchange, with secure wallet linking, real-time exchange status and trader-to-trader messaging.

Key features

  • Peer-to-peer exchange
  • Secure wallet linking
  • Live exchange status
  • Trader messaging

The outcome

Coines shipped to iOS and Android, bringing simple P2P crypto exchange to its market.
