Spotly: find the day out, keep the memory.

Spotly answers the question every parent in Kuwait asks on a Thursday: where do we take the kids? It finds places worth going, and keeps the photos from the ones you loved.

ClientKhatib Designs (own product)
CountryKuwait
Launched2026
PlatformiOS, web

The challenge

The answer to "what is on this weekend" was scattered across Instagram stories, WhatsApp groups and out-of-date listings, and almost none of it worked properly in Arabic. The information existed. Nothing collected it, screened it, or made it searchable in both languages.

What we built

A bilingual discovery app with places screened by an AI pass before they are published, so listings stay accurate without a manual review queue.

A merchant side lets venues manage their own listings, vouchers and offers. The whole product mirrors properly between Arabic and English rather than being translated into right-to-left.

Key features

  • Arabic and English, both first-class
  • AI place screening
  • Merchant listings and vouchers
  • Saved outings and photo memories

The outcome

Spotly is live on the App Store, and its guides now rank on Google, with the parks guide drawing most of that traffic. The discovery problem it was built to solve is now sending it visitors.
