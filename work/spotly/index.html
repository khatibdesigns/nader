The challenge

The answer to "what is on this weekend" was scattered across Instagram stories, WhatsApp groups and out-of-date listings — and almost none of it worked properly in Arabic. The information existed; nothing collected it, screened it, or made it searchable in both languages.

What we built

A bilingual discovery app with places screened by an AI pass before they are published, so listings stay accurate without a manual review queue. A merchant side lets venues manage their own listings, vouchers and offers, and the whole product mirrors properly between Arabic and English rather than being translated into RTL.

Key features

  • Arabic and English, both first-class
  • AI place screening
  • Merchant listings and vouchers
  • Saved outings and photo memories

The outcome

Spotly is live on the App Store, and its content now ranks: in the last 90 days its guides drew over 18,000 impressions on Google at an average position of six, with the parks guide alone accounting for most of that. The discovery problem it was built to solve is now sending it traffic.
