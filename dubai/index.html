FAQ

Dubai & the UAE, answered.

How much does app development cost in Dubai?

It depends on scope. A focused MVP typically starts in the low five figures, while a full multi-platform product with backend, integrations and AI features costs more. We scope to your budget and tell you honestly what a strong first version needs.

Do you work with clients in Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, not just Dubai?

Yes. We work with businesses across the whole UAE — Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the other emirates — as well as the wider GCC. Delivery is the same wherever you're based.

Do you work remotely or on-site in the UAE?

Primarily remotely, which keeps timelines fast and costs efficient, and we meet on-site or by video when a project calls for it. We've shipped products across six countries this way.

Can you build bilingual Arabic & English apps for the UAE market?

Yes — bilingual EN/AR delivery is core to how we work. We design proper right-to-left Arabic experiences that feel native, not translated, alongside the English version.
