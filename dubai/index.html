Why Arabic matters

Most AI tooling is built English-first. Gulf Arabic brings dialect, tone, right-to-left layout and the way people actually type on their phones. An assistant that gets these wrong sounds like a bad translation of your brand.

We are bilingual and build in both languages from the first screen. Our agents and document pipelines are tested on real Arabic and English input before your customers see them.
