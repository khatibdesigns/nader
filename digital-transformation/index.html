Digital transformation that your teams keep using.

We modernise how your organisation runs: processes, data, systems and customer channels, delivered in stages that each pay for themselves. Led by Nader Alkhatib.

What we change.

Transformation fails when it is a slide deck or a single big launch. We change one part of the business at a time and keep the rest running.

Processes

We map how work really moves through the business today, where it waits, and where people re-type the same thing twice. Then we redesign it around the system that will carry it.

  • Current-state review
  • Process redesign
  • Roles and handovers

Systems and data

Old tools, spreadsheets and paper move onto modern systems, and the data from all of them comes into one place you can trust and report on.

  • System selection or custom build
  • Migration from legacy tools
  • One source of truth for reporting

Customer and staff channels

Mobile apps, web portals and self-service that let customers and staff do in minutes what used to take a phone call or a visit.

  • Mobile and web apps
  • Portals and self-service
  • Payments and notifications

Change and adoption

A new system only counts once people use it. We train teams, run the old and new side by side where needed, and measure adoption, not launch dates.

  • Training and documentation
  • Phased roll-out
  • Adoption measured after launch

Where we have done it.

Transformation work our founder and our team have led.

  • A European bank

    Nader led engineering teams of up to 25 developers on the bank's digital transformation: its mobile banking app, digital lending and online customer onboarding.

  • A college in Lebanon

    School Universal College moved its website, admissions, student records and accounts onto one platform we built, in Arabic, English and French, with its own app for students and parents.

  • A tailor in Kuwait

    Years of customers and orders moved into MODAX, our business system, with no lost records, and it stays in sync with the shop's existing tools.

  • A US medical group

    For five years we were ABS Medical's technology team: iOS apps connected to their health records, and support whenever something needed fixing.

And where AI fits.

Most AI projects stall on old processes and scattered data. Transformation is what makes AI possible later: once work runs on modern systems, an agent can do the repetitive part of it. We plan both together, so nothing is built twice.
