In short.

Nader has more than 13 years in engineering and technology leadership, leading organisations of hundreds of engineers and helping businesses reach their goals, from strategy and consulting to raising investment. His first AI system in production was a computer-vision scale that tracks food waste for a US startup, in 2017. Today his company runs on AI agents his team built, and the same agents are what Khatib Designs builds for clients.

He holds a degree in computer engineering and an Executive MBA from the University of York, and he speaks Arabic, English and Bulgarian.
