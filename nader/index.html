What I actually believe

The gap is never the model.

Almost every stalled AI project I have seen in the Gulf stalled for the same three reasons, and none of them were technical: nobody agreed which decisions AI was allowed to make, nobody owned it when it was wrong, and nobody had decided what to build first.

The second thing: Arabic is not a translation layer. A product that reads as translated reads as untrustworthy, and in this region that decides adoption long before the technology does.

And the third: a pilot that cannot go live is not a pilot, it is a slide. I would rather ship one working agent in two weeks than present a roadmap for six.
