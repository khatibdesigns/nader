How accurate is the estimate?

It is an honest range, not a quote. It is built from what we have charged for comparable work, and in practice a scoping call moves the final figure inside the range far more often than outside it. What it will never do is show you a number we would not stand behind.

Why is the range so wide?

Because scope is the price. Two apps that sound identical in a sentence can differ threefold once you count user roles, integrations and who has to approve what. The range narrows the moment we see your actual workflows — that is the whole point of the call.

Do I have to give you my details to see it?

No. Nothing is sent anywhere when you press the button; the maths runs in your browser. If the number works for you, book a call or send the summary to us on WhatsApp — and if it does not, you have saved us both a meeting.
