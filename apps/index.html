Questions, answered.

If yours isn't here, ask it on the call.

Who owns the app?

You do. Every app is published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, so the store listing, the reviews and the customers are yours. On custom apps you also own the design and the code once the project is paid.

What is the monthly fee for on a Ready App?

KWD 29 a month covers hosting, keeping the app working on every new iOS and Android version, and support. On Ready App + Website the same KWD 29 covers the website too. The minimum term is 12 months; after that you can cancel any time.

Can I start with a Ready App and upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade within 6 months and the full setup fee (KWD 500 or KWD 1,000) is credited toward Starter or above. Many businesses start ready, learn what their customers actually use, then build custom.

How long does it take?

A Ready App, with or without the website, is live in 4 to 6 weeks once we have your logo, colours and content. Starter takes six to eight weeks, Growth usually two to three months and Premium three to four, depending on scope.

Do you build websites too?

Yes. Ready App + Website (KWD 1,000 setup + KWD 29/month) adds an online store or booking website on your own domain, running on the same products, orders and customers as your app, with one dashboard for both. Need a fully custom website or web app? We quote it after a free call.

Does the price include the website?

The KWD 500 Ready App is the app only. Ready App + Website includes the website in the KWD 1,000 setup, and the same KWD 29 a month covers hosting, updates and support for the app and the website together. The domain name stays registered in your name.

Can customers pay inside the app?

Yes, from Growth up: KNET, Apple Pay and cards through MyFatoorah or Tap. Payments go straight to your own merchant account.

Is the Arabic done properly?

Yes. Every app ships in Arabic and English with a full right-to-left layout, Arabic typography and copy written by native speakers, never machine-translated.
